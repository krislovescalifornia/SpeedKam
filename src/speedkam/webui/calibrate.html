Calibrate (no tape measure)

  1. Pick a known, steady speed you can hold past the camera — a phone GPS speedo works. Note it in mph or km/h.
  2. Drive past once each direction at that speed while the node is running.
  3. Read the node's log for the crossing time it printed for each pass: 
    journalctl -u speedkam | grep CALIBRATE
    e.g. CALIBRATE Eastbound: crossed x1000->450 in 0.512s.
  4. Turn each into a distance below, then put the two numbers in config.local.yaml and restart: 
    speed:
  d_east_m: <east result>
  d_west_m: <west result>
